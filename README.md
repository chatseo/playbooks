- [Claude Runs Your SEO](https://chatseo.github.io/playbooks/claude-runs-your-seo/) — connect Claude to your Search Console and your CMS: the 7 jobs, from finding the quick win to shipping and verifying the fix.
- [The Claude SEO System](https://chatseo.github.io/playbooks/claude-seo-system/) — find the pages Google already wants to rank, fix them, recheck on autopilot.
- [The Claude SEO Department](https://chatseo.github.io/playbooks/claude-seo-department/) — six SEO functions, one shared brief.
