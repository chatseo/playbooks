# Check whether AI engines cite you

**Area:** AI search visibility · **Time:** 30 min · **Skill:** `citation-checker`

**Goal.** Find out, for your key questions, whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude mention or cite your brand, and record it.

**When.** You want a baseline of AI visibility before you change anything, or a monthly re-check.

## You need

- your 20 key questions, the ones a buyer would ask before choosing a product like yours
- a spreadsheet with one row per question and one column per AI engine

## Steps

1. Run each of the 20 questions in each engine in a fresh session, and paste the full answer, with any sources shown, into a text file labelled with the question and the engine.
2. In the sheet, record per cell whether your brand was named, whether a URL of yours was cited, and which competitors were named, straight from the answers.
3. Paste the sheet and the answer file into Claude and ask it to summarise, per engine, the share of questions where you are named, where you are cited, and who is named most.
4. Claude returns the summary table from the counts given, the list of questions where no engine names you, and the competitor that appears most often, without extrapolating beyond the 20.
5. Save the sheet with the date as this month's baseline and put the questions where nobody names you at the top of the answer content queue.

**Done when a dated baseline sheet exists for all 20 questions across the engines and the unnamed questions are queued.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
