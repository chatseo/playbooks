# Check AI engines describe your brand correctly

**Area:** AI search visibility · **Time:** 30 min · **Skill:** `entity-consistency`

**Goal.** Find every wrong fact AI engines state about your brand, trace each to its likely source, and correct the source.

**When.** You have never asked the engines what they think you are, or your pricing, positioning or founders changed.

## You need

- the official brand facts: what you do, who it is for, pricing tiers, founders, founding year, headquarters, integrations
- the answers from four AI engines to: what is BRAND, what does BRAND cost, who is BRAND for, and BRAND alternatives
- the pages you control that state those facts: about page, pricing page, LinkedIn and Crunchbase profiles, Wikipedia if any

## Steps

1. Run the four questions in each engine in a fresh session and save every answer with the sources shown, one file per engine.
2. Paste the official facts and the answer files into Claude and ask it to list every statement that contradicts the facts, with the engine and the exact wording.
3. Paste the text of your controlled pages and ask Claude to point, per wrong statement, to the controlled page that is silent or outdated on that fact, or to say no controlled source.
4. Claude returns wrong statement, engine, the likely stale or missing source among your pages, and the exact sentence to add or change on that page.
5. Update your controlled pages first, then the third-party profiles you can edit, and rerun the four questions in a month.

**Done when every wrong statement has a corrected source page and a one-month recheck is scheduled.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
