# Write a brief a writer cannot make generic

**Area:** Content strategy and briefs · **Time:** 45 min · **Skill:** `brief-builder`

**Goal.** Produce a content brief with enough specifics that the draft, human or AI, cannot fall back to generic filler.

**When.** You are about to hand a target keyword to a writer or to Claude and the last brief produced mush.

## You need

- the target keyword and the cluster it belongs to
- the top 5 ranking URLs for the keyword, pasted as text or as a page-by-page notes file
- three things your company knows about the topic that the ranking pages do not say

## Steps

1. Open the top 5 results, copy their H2s and any numbers they cite into one notes file, and write down what every one of them repeats and what none of them covers.
2. Paste the notes file, the keyword and your three proprietary points into Claude and ask for a brief with a stated reader, a stated promise, and the sections in order.
3. Ask Claude to attach to every section one concrete requirement, a number, an example, a screenshot, or a named source, and to mark sections with no such requirement as at risk of filler.
4. Claude returns the brief with a word target per section, the entities and terms that must appear, and the list of claims the writer must not make without a source.
5. Cut every section marked at risk of filler unless you can supply the missing specific yourself, then send the brief.

**Done when every section in the brief carries at least one concrete requirement and no section is marked at risk of filler.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
