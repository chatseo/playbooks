# Prospect broken pages you could replace

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Time:** 1 h · **Skill:** `broken-link-prospector`

**Goal.** Find dead pages on your topic that still receive links, so you can offer a working replacement to the sites linking to them.

**When.** You have a strong resource page and want links from sites that already link to something like it.

## You need

- a broken backlinks or broken pages export from your backlink tool for three competitor domains or for your topic
- the list of linking pages for each broken URL, with anchor text
- your resource pages that could replace each broken one

## Steps

1. Export the broken pages with their referring domain counts, keep the ones with at least 5 referring domains, and pull the linking pages for each.
2. Fetch each broken URL from the Wayback Machine and copy a short summary of what the page used to be, so you know what a replacement must cover.
3. Paste the broken URLs, their summaries and your resource pages into Claude and ask it to match each broken page to your closest replacement, or to write no match.
4. Claude returns broken URL, referring domain count, the matching page of yours and the gap between the two that you would need to close before pitching.
5. For the top 10 matches, close the gap where it is small, then queue the linking pages for outreach with the broken URL and your replacement side by side.

**Done when ten broken pages have a matched replacement and their linking pages are queued for outreach.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
