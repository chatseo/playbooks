# Find sites linking to competitors, not you

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Time:** 1 h · **Skill:** `link-gap`

**Goal.** Build a shortlist of domains that link to two or more competitors and not to you, ranked by how winnable each looks.

**When.** You are starting a link building push and want targets that already link to sites like yours.

## You need

- a link intersect export from Ahrefs or Semrush for three competitors versus your domain, with referring domain, domain rating and the linked competitor URLs
- a one-paragraph description of your site and the content you could offer a linking site

## Steps

1. Run the intersect for three competitors, export it, and keep only domains that link to at least two of the three, with the columns for the linking page and its anchor.
2. Paste the export into Claude with your site description and ask it to tag each domain by link type from the linking page URL and anchor: resource list, guest article, directory, news mention or other.
3. Ask Claude to drop directories and paid-looking domains and to rank the rest by how likely they are to link to a site like yours, with a one-line reason per domain.
4. Claude returns the ranked shortlist with the type, the competitor page they linked to, and the page of yours that could replace it, or none if you have nothing comparable.
5. Take the top 25 with a page of yours attached, put them in your outreach sheet, and leave the none rows as content ideas for later.

**Done when 25 target domains sit in your outreach sheet, each with a link type and a page of yours to pitch.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
