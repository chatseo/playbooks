# Turn your data into a PR story

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Time:** 1 h · **Skill:** `digital-pr-angle`

**Goal.** Find the one angle in your own data that a journalist on your beat would cover, and shape it into a pitchable story.

**When.** You have proprietary data, usage numbers, survey results or a dataset, and no story around it yet.

## You need

- the dataset or the summary table, with the sample size and the date range
- five recent headlines from the publications you want to reach, on your topic
- one sentence on what your company is not allowed to claim

## Steps

1. Write down the five most surprising numbers in the data, each with its sample size, and note anything you cannot claim for legal or accuracy reasons.
2. Paste the numbers, the sample sizes, the headlines and the restriction into Claude and ask for the angles a journalist writing those headlines would run.
3. Ask Claude to rank the angles by newsworthiness and to reject any angle that the sample size cannot support, saying why.
4. Claude returns the top angle with a draft headline, the three numbers that carry it, the one chart to make, and the caveat a journalist would ask about.
5. Make the chart from your own numbers, write the two-paragraph pitch around the headline, and pick the five journalists whose headlines matched.

**Done when one angle has a headline, a chart, a pitch and five named journalists.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
