# Audit a Google Business Profile

**Area:** Local SEO · **Time:** 30 min · **Skill:** `gbp-audit`

**Goal.** Check every field of one Google Business Profile against the business's reality and its top local competitor, and fix what is missing.

**When.** You are taking on a local client or the profile has not been touched in a year.

## You need

- every field of the profile copied into a text file: name, categories, address, hours, phone, website, description, services, attributes, photo count, post dates, review count and rating
- the same fields for the competitor that ranks first in the map pack for your main service
- the business's real services, hours and locations from the owner

## Steps

1. Copy every profile field into a file, then open the competitor's profile and copy the same fields, so the comparison is line by line.
2. Paste both files and the owner's real services list into Claude and ask it to flag every field that is empty, outdated, different from the owner's facts, or weaker than the competitor's.
3. Ask Claude to name the primary category the business should use and to list the services from the owner's list that are missing from the profile.
4. Claude returns a checklist ordered by likely impact: the field, what is there now, what should be there, and the source of the correction, owner facts or competitor gap.
5. Fix the fields in the profile in that order, upload at least five recent photos if the count is low, and set a monthly reminder to post and reply.

**Done when every flagged field has been corrected in the profile and a monthly maintenance reminder exists.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
