# Build a location page that is not thin

**Area:** Local SEO · **Time:** 1 h · **Skill:** `local-landing-page-builder`

**Goal.** Write one location page with enough real local detail that it could not be swapped for another city's page.

**When.** You serve several areas and the location pages you have are the same text with the city name changed.

## You need

- the location: address or service area, the team who covers it, and the services actually offered there
- local specifics from the owner: recent jobs in that area, local regulations, landmarks customers mention, area-specific pricing or hours
- three reviews from customers in that area, with permission to quote

## Steps

1. Interview the owner or the local lead for 10 minutes and write down every area-specific fact: jobs done, streets or neighbourhoods served, local rules, seasonal issues.
2. Paste the facts, the services list and the three reviews into Claude and ask for a page outline where every section carries at least one fact that is true only for this location.
3. Ask Claude to write the page from the outline, and to write NEEDS LOCAL FACT wherever a section would otherwise fall back on generic text.
4. Claude returns the page with the placeholders listed, plus a suggested title and H1 that include the service and the location without stuffing.
5. Fill the placeholders from the owner or cut those sections, add the reviews with names and area, and link the page from the main services page and the footer.

**Done when the page is live with zero placeholders and every section holds at least one fact specific to that location.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
