# Work out why a competitor wins the map pack

**Area:** Local SEO · **Time:** 45 min · **Skill:** `local-pack-gap`

**Goal.** Compare your profile, site and reviews against the competitor that beats you in the map pack, and pick the one gap to close first.

**When.** A competitor holds the map pack for your main service and you sit below the pack or in fourth place.

## You need

- your profile fields and the competitor's, side by side: primary category, review count, rating, review velocity over 90 days, photo count, services listed, website link
- the homepage title, H1 and first paragraph of both websites
- your positions for the main service query from three points in the service area, from a local rank tracker or a manual check

## Steps

1. Fill a two-column sheet with the profile fields for both businesses, then add the homepage title, H1 and first paragraph of each site below.
2. Add your three-point ranking check and the distance from each check point to both businesses, if you know the addresses.
3. Paste the sheet into Claude and ask it to list every field where the competitor is stronger, with the size of the gap taken from the sheet, and to leave proximity out unless the addresses are given.
4. Claude returns the gaps ranked by size, and names the single one to close first among category, reviews, site relevance or services, with the reason in one line.
5. Close that one gap over the next 30 days, log the starting positions, and rerun the three-point check at the end of the month.

**Done when one named gap is being worked with a 30-day plan and the starting positions are logged.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
