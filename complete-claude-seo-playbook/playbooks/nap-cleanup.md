# Fix NAP inconsistencies across the web

**Area:** Local SEO · **Time:** 1 h · **Skill:** `nap-consistency`

**Goal.** Find every place online where the business name, address or phone differs from the official version, and correct the ones that matter.

**When.** The business moved, changed its phone number, or rebranded, or its map pack rankings dropped for no clear reason.

## You need

- the official name, address and phone, exactly as on the Google Business Profile
- a citation export from a local SEO tool, or a manual list of listings found by searching the old phone number and address in quotes

## Steps

1. Search the old phone number in quotes, then the old address, then the business name with the city, and record every listing found with its URL and the exact name, address and phone shown.
2. Paste the list and the official version into Claude and ask it to mark each listing as exact match, formatting difference only, or real inconsistency, field by field.
3. Ask Claude to order the real inconsistencies by how likely the site matters: the major aggregators, industry directories, then the rest.
4. Claude returns the ordered list with the listing URL, the wrong field, the correct value, and marks listings it could not read as needs manual check.
5. Correct the top listings yourself through each site's edit or claim process, and log the submission date so you can verify the change in a month.

**Done when every real inconsistency on a major aggregator or industry directory has a correction submitted and logged.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
