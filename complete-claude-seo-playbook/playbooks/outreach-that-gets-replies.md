# Write outreach emails that get replies

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Time:** 45 min · **Skill:** `outreach-writer`

**Goal.** Write short, specific outreach emails for a batch of link targets, each referencing the page you want the link on.

**When.** You have an outreach sheet with targets and pages, and the template emails are getting no replies.

## You need

- the outreach sheet: contact name, site, the exact page you want the link on, and the page of yours to add
- one sentence per target on why your page improves theirs, written by you
- the last three emails you sent that got no reply

## Steps

1. Paste the three unanswered emails into Claude and ask it to list what they have in common that a busy editor would delete on sight, such as length or generic praise.
2. Paste the sheet in batches of 10 and ask Claude to write one email per row, under 90 words, opening with the target page and the specific gap your page closes.
3. Tell Claude that every email must contain the one-sentence reason you wrote and no claim about the target's site that is not in the sheet.
4. Claude returns subject line and body per row, with the ask as one plain question, and flags rows where the reason you gave was too weak to build on.
5. Send the batch from a real address, log the send date per row, and rewrite the flagged reasons before sending those.

**Done when every row in the batch has a sent email under 90 words with a specific reason and a logged date.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
