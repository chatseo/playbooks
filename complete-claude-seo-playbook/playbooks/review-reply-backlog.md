# Answer a backlog of reviews

**Area:** Local SEO · **Time:** 30 min · **Skill:** `review-response-writer`

**Goal.** Reply to every unanswered review in one sitting with responses that are specific to each review and useful to the next reader.

**When.** The profile has unanswered reviews older than a week, especially negative ones.

## You need

- the unanswered reviews exported or copied, with rating, date, reviewer first name and the full text
- the business's reply rules: who signs, what may never be promised, and how refunds or complaints are handled
- the services list, so replies can name the service the reviewer used

## Steps

1. Copy every unanswered review into a file with its rating and date, oldest first, and write the reply rules at the top.
2. Paste the file into Claude in batches of 15 and ask it to write one reply per review under 80 words that references a detail from the review and names the service where it can.
3. Tell Claude that negative replies must acknowledge the specific complaint, offer one next step from the rules, and never argue or promise what the rules forbid.
4. Claude returns the replies with the detail it referenced highlighted, and flags reviews where it could not find a specific detail to reference, or that look like they need the owner.
5. Post the replies, rewriting the flagged ones yourself, and start with the negative reviews since those are what prospects read first.

**Done when every review in the batch has a posted reply that references something specific from it.**

## Rules

- Claude never states a number it was not given. It writes "cannot compute from this data" instead of estimating.
- Every step ends on one action, not a list of options.
