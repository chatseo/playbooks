# Build a content brief from the SERP

**Area:** Content strategy and briefs

**When.** Paste this when a writer needs a brief for one keyword and you have copied the top 10 titles and headings.

## Prompt

```
Below are: my target keyword, the titles and H2 headings of the top 10 ranking pages copied from the SERP, and who the reader is. If any of these is missing, ask me for it before doing anything.

Write a brief for a writer who does not know SEO. Include: the search intent in one sentence; the angle that separates this page from the 10 pasted (something at most 2 of them cover); a target length as a range taken from the pasted pages only, and if I did not give their lengths write "cannot compute from this data"; a heading outline with H2s and H3s, max 8 H2s; entities and terms to include, max 15; questions to answer, max 6; internal pages to link (ask for my sitemap if needed); what to avoid, max 4 items taken from the top 10's weaknesses.

Use exactly these sections in this order: Intent, Angle, Length, Outline, Entities, Questions, Internal links, Avoid. Never estimate a number that is not in the data.

Finish with one action: the single section the writer should draft first, no alternatives.
```
