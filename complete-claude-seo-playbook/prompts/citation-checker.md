# Check my citations across AI engines

**Area:** AI search visibility

**When.** Paste this with the answers you collected from AI engines when you want a citation rate instead of a feeling.

## Prompt

```
Below are full answers from AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Claude) for a set of prompts, one block per engine and prompt, with the sources each answer showed, plus my domain. If answers, sources or my domain are missing, ask me for them before doing anything.

For each prompt and engine, record whether my domain is cited (a link or a named mention), which page, its position among the sources, and which competitor domains are cited. Count only what is in the pasted answers; do not infer anything about prompts I did not paste. Citation rate per engine is prompts citing me divided by prompts checked.

Output a table, max 30 rows, columns: prompt, engine, my domain cited (yes or no), my page, source position, competitors cited (max 3). Then a summary table: engine, prompts checked, prompts citing me, citation rate, competitor cited most. If a number is not in the data I gave you, write "cannot compute from this data". Never estimate.

Finish with one action: the single prompt where I am closest to being cited and what to change, no alternatives.
```
