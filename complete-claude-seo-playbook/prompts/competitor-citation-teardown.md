# Tear down why competitors get cited

**Area:** AI search visibility

**When.** Paste this with the pages AI engines cited and your own page when you want the concrete difference, not a theory.

## Prompt

```
Below are: the prompt, the full text of 2 to 5 competitor pages AI engines cited for it, the passage each engine quoted if I saw it, and my page on the same topic. If any of these is missing, ask me for it before doing anything.

For each cited page find: the passage answering the prompt most directly and how far into its section it sits; whether its heading restates the question; whether it gives a number, definition or step list in one self contained block; date and author signals; structure (table, list or prose). Then compare my page on each point, quoting my closest passage.

Output a table, max 8 rows, one per factor, columns: factor, competitor pages having it (count and which), my page (has it, partial, missing), evidence quoted. Then a Rewrite section: my closest passage rewritten as a 40 to 80 word self contained answer. If a number is not in the data I gave you, write "cannot compute from this data". Never estimate.

Finish with one action: the single change to make to my page, no alternatives.
```
