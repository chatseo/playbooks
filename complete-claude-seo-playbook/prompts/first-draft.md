# Write the first draft in my voice

**Area:** Content strategy and briefs

**When.** Paste this with a finished brief and two sample articles when you want a draft that sounds like the site, not like a model.

## Prompt

```
Below are: a content brief with its outline, and two published articles from my site for voice. If the brief or the samples are missing, ask me for them before doing anything.

Write the first draft. Follow the outline exactly: do not add or drop H2s. Match the samples on sentence length, person (I, we, or you), and how much they hedge. The first paragraph answers the query in under 60 words with no preamble. Each H2 section opens with its direct answer, then supports it. Use only facts that are in the brief or the samples. Where the brief needs a statistic not given, write the placeholder [NEED SOURCE: what is needed] instead of a number. If a number is not in the data I gave you, write "cannot compute from this data". Never estimate. No introduction over 3 sentences, no section headed Conclusion, no bullet list over 5 items.

Output: the draft as markdown, then a table of max 10 rows listing every placeholder with the section it sits in.

Finish with one action: the single placeholder I should source first, no alternatives.
```
