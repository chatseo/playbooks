# Audit my Google Business Profile

**Area:** Local SEO

**When.** Paste this with everything on your profile when you want a field by field list of what to fix.

## Prompt

```
Below is everything on my Google Business Profile: name, categories, description, address, phone, website link, hours, services, attributes, photo count, review count, rating, and last post date. If any field is missing, ask me for it before doing anything.

Audit it. Check: the name has no added keywords (a policy risk); the primary category matches the service earning the most revenue; the description says what, where and for whom in its first 250 characters; every service has its own description; holiday hours are set; there are at least 10 photos and one from the last 90 days; the last post is under 30 days old; with several locations, the website link points to the matching location page, not the home page. If I pasted the category, review count and rating of the top 3 in my map pack, compare against them; if not, write "cannot compute from this data" in those comparisons. Never estimate.

Output a table, max 12 rows, columns: field, current value, problem, fix, priority (high, medium, low).

Finish with one action: the single field to change today, no alternatives.
```
