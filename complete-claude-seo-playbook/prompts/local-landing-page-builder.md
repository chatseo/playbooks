# Write a location landing page

**Area:** Local SEO

**When.** Paste this with real local facts and quotes when you need a location page that is not a doorway page.

## Prompt

```
Below are: the service, the area, what makes this location different (team, address, hours, parking, local projects), 2 or 3 real customer quotes from the area, and my existing service page URL. If the local facts or quotes are missing, ask me for them before doing anything; without them this is a doorway page.

Write the location page, under 700 words. Structure: H1 with service and area; a first paragraph under 80 words stating the service, the area covered and one local fact; how the service works here; local proof (the quotes and projects, neighbourhood named); the neighbourhoods served, only the ones I gave; an FAQ of max 4 local questions; a contact block with address, phone and hours. Do not invent projects, quotes, staff names or distances. If a number is not in the data I gave you, write "cannot compute from this data". Never estimate.

Output: the page in markdown, then a title tag under 60 characters and a meta description under 155 characters.

Finish with one action: the single local fact I should add to make it stronger, no alternatives.
```
