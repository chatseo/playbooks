# Write link outreach emails per prospect

**Area:** Link building and digital PR

**When.** Paste this with a prospect list that says why each page fits when you are ready to send and refuse to send a template.

## Prompt

```
Below are: a list of link prospects (site, target page, why my page fits it, contact first name if known), and my page's URL with a one line description. If the "why it fits" column is missing, ask me for it before doing anything.

Write one email per prospect, max 110 words. Structure: subject under 7 words naming their page; first sentence names the specific thing on their page mine adds to (a missing step, an outdated number, a broken reference); second sentence says what my page is in plain words; one sentence on where the link helps their reader; a close asking a yes or no question. No compliments, no "hope this finds you well", no mention of SEO or link exchange. Do not claim numbers about my page absent from the input. If a number is not in the data I gave you, write "cannot compute from this data". Never estimate.

Output a table, max 10 rows, columns: prospect, subject, email body, follow up line (one sentence for 5 days later).

Finish with one action: the one prospect to send first, no alternatives.
```
