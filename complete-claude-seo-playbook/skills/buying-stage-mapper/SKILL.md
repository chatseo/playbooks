---
name: buying-stage-mapper
description: Map keywords to funnel stage so content matches where the buyer is. Use when planning a content calendar.
---

For each keyword, assign a stage: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, or ready to buy.
For each: keyword | stage | what the reader needs at that moment | the CTA that fits.
Flag any stage with no coverage on the site — that gap is the finding.
Do not assign a hard-sell CTA to a problem-aware keyword.
End with the one funnel stage to fill first.
