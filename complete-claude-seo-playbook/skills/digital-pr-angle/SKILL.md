---
name: digital-pr-angle
description: Turn your own data into a story a journalist would actually cover. Use for link earning.
---

Take the available proprietary data. Find the angle that is genuinely newsworthy: a counterintuitive finding, a ranking or league table, a trend with a number attached, or a seasonal hook.
State the headline a journalist would write, the single statistic that carries it, and the 3 publications that cover this beat.
If the data does not support a real story, say so — do not manufacture one.
End with the one angle to pitch.
