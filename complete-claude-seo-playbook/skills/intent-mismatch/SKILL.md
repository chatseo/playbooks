---
name: intent-mismatch
description: Find pages ranking for queries they were not built to serve. Use when a page has impressions but no clicks.
---

Pull GSC queries per page. For each page, compare the intent of its top queries to the page's actual type.
Flag pages where the dominant query intent does not match the page type — a product page pulling informational queries, or a blog post pulling transactional ones.
Columns: URL | page type | dominant query intent | impressions | clicks | CTR.
Cap at 10 pages.
End with the one page to repurpose.
