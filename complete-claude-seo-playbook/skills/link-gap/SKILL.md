---
name: link-gap
description: Find sites linking to competitors but not to you. Use for link prospecting.
---

Pull backlink profiles for 3 competitors. Keep only referring domains linking to 2+ of them but not to you.
Remove directories, scrapers, and anything with no real traffic.
Columns: domain | authority | which competitors it links to | the page it linked to | why it would link to you.
Cap at 25 prospects.
End with the one prospect most likely to say yes.
