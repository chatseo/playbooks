---
name: link-opportunity-finder
description: Find existing pages that should link to your money page. Use to push a specific URL.
---

Take the target URL. Find every page on the site that mentions its topic or ranks for a related query but does not link to it.
For each, quote the exact sentence where the link belongs and propose the anchor text.
Columns: source page | its authority signal | sentence to link from | proposed anchor.
Cap at 15 opportunities, sorted by the source page's own traffic.
End with the single highest-value link to add.
