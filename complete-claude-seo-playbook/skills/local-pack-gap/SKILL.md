description: Work out why a competitor outranks you in the map pack. Use when local rankings stall.
---

Compare your GBP to the top 3 map pack competitors on: primary category, review count, average rating, review recency, photo count, post frequency, proximity to the searcher, and website relevance for the query.
Columns: signal | you | competitor 1 | competitor 2 | competitor 3 | gap.
Proximity cannot be changed — if that is the whole gap, say so honestly.
End with the one closable gap.
