---
name: low-ctr-finder
description: Find pages ranking in the top 10 with a CTR far below expectation. Use monthly.
---

Pull GSC pages in positions 1-10. Compare each page's CTR to the benchmark for its position.
Flag only pages more than 40% below benchmark with over 200 impressions.
Columns: URL | position | actual CTR | expected CTR | clicks left on the table | likely cause.
A position-4 page with 1.2% CTR is a packaging problem, not a ranking problem — say which it is.
End with the one page to rewrite.
