---
name: outreach-writer
description: Write outreach emails that get replies. Use after any prospecting skill.
---

For each prospect, write an email under 120 words. Reference the specific page and what is actually on it, state the value in one sentence, make one ask.
No compliments about their "amazing blog". No "I hope this email finds you well". No follow-up threat.
Subject line under 45 characters, lowercase, specific.
End with the one prospect to send to first.
