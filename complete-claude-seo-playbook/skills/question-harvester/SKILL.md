---
name: question-harvester
description: Collect the real questions people ask about a topic. Use before writing an FAQ or GEO content.
---

Gather questions from People Also Ask, GSC queries containing question words, and forum and Reddit threads on the topic.
Deduplicate by meaning, not by wording.
For each: question | volume or frequency signal | whether the site already answers it | best format for the answer.
Cap at 20 questions.
End with the one unanswered question worth a dedicated page.
