**To:** [name, role]  **Their page:** [URL where the link would fit]
**Our asset:** [URL]  **The specific fit:** [what on their page our asset completes or fixes]

## Touch 1, day 0

**Subject:** [their page title or topic, 4-7 words, no "quick question"]

Hi [first name],

On [their page title] you [link to / mention / recommend] [the thing]. [One sentence on what is missing or outdated there, stated as a fact you checked, with the date].

We published [asset title]: [one sentence on what it adds, with a number if you have one].

If it fits, [the exact place on their page where it would go]. If not, no reply needed.

[Your name]
[Role, site]

## Touch 2, day 5

**Subject:** Re: [same subject]

Hi [first name], one more note in case the first got buried. The short version: [their page] [has the gap], [our asset] [fills it]. Happy to send [a summary / the data / an image] if that is easier than reading the page.

[Your name]

## Touch 3, day 12

**Subject:** Re: [same subject]

Hi [first name], last note from me. If [asset] is not a fit for [their page], I will leave it there. If there is someone else who edits that section, I would be grateful for a name.

Thanks either way,
[Your name]

## Rules

- Every bracket is filled with something you checked on their page. If the first paragraph could be sent to any site, rewrite it.
- No attachments, no tracking pixels, no "I hope this finds you well".
- Log every touch and result in the prospect sheet.
