# The decay rescue

**Area:** Content strategy and briefs · **Cadence:** when a page decays · **Time:** 3 h

**Trigger.** A page that used to bring clicks loses 30 percent or more over two consecutive 28-day windows.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Export Search Console pages for the last 3 months compared with the previous 3 months. Paste it and ask which pages lost the most clicks with stable impressions. | `decay-detector` | A ranked table of decaying pages with the click delta and the top lost query. |
| 2 | For the page you are rescuing, paste the current top 10 for its main query. Ask what format ranks now and whether it changed. | `serp-shape-reader` | The ranking format, length and angle, and what your page lacks. |
| 3 | Hand it the page text, the lost queries and the SERP reading. Ask for a rewrite plan that keeps what still ranks and replaces what does not. | `content-refresh` | A section-level rewrite plan with keep, cut and add per block. |
| 4 | Rewrite in the CMS following the plan. Keep the URL, update the date only if the content really changed, and submit for indexing. | manual | The refreshed page live under the same URL. |
| 5 | Wait 28 days, re-export the same comparison and check the page's clicks and the lost queries' positions. | manual | A before and after row for the page. |

**Result.** A decaying page rewritten against what currently ranks, kept on its URL, with a dated before and after row so you know whether the rescue worked.
