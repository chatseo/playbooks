# The monthly AI citation check

**Area:** AI search visibility · **Cadence:** monthly · **Time:** 2 h

**Trigger.** Mid-month, on the 20 queries that matter most to your business.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run your 20 core queries in the AI engines you care about and paste each answer with its cited sources. Ask which cite you, which cite competitors. | `citation-checker` | A table: query, engine, cited or not, who is cited instead. |
| 2 | For the queries where a competitor is cited and you are not, paste the cited competitor passage and your page's closest passage. | `competitor-citation-teardown` | Per query, what the cited passage does that yours does not. |
| 3 | Paste your page and the teardown notes. Ask for a self-contained passage that answers the query directly, with a fact and a source in it. | `answer-block-writer` | One citable passage per losing query, with a placement suggestion. |
| 4 | Add the passages to the pages in the CMS, near the top or under the matching heading, and note the date per page. | manual | Updated pages with a ship date. |
| 5 | Next month, rerun the same 20 queries and compare the citation table row by row. | manual | Citation table before and after. |

**Result.** A monthly citation table for your 20 queries, one citable passage shipped per lost query, and the row-by-row comparison that shows whether AI answers picked it up.
