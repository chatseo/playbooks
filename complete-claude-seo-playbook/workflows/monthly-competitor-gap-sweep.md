# The monthly competitor gap sweep

**Area:** Keyword research and clustering · **Cadence:** monthly · **Time:** 90 min

**Trigger.** First Monday of the month, or the day a new competitor shows up in your Search Console queries.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Export the organic keywords of your 3 closest competitors from Ahrefs or Semrush, and your own top queries from Search Console for the same 28 days. | manual | Four CSVs: three competitor keyword lists and your own query list. |
| 2 | Paste all four exports and ask for the queries at least two competitors rank for that you do not appear for at all. | `keyword-gap` | A gap table: query, which competitors rank, their positions, your absence. |
| 3 | Run the gap table through so every query is tagged by buying stage. Ask it to flag stages where you have no page at all. | `buying-stage-mapper` | The gap table with a stage column and the empty stages named. |
| 4 | Keep only the bottom-of-funnel and comparison queries you can credibly answer. Drop anything that would need a product you do not have. | manual | A shortlist of 5 to 10 gap queries with the stage and a proposed page. |
| 5 | Add the shortlist to the content calendar with the competitor URL that ranks as the reference for the brief. | manual | Calendar entries with a reference URL each. |

**Result.** A monthly list of competitor-proven queries you are missing, each with a stage and a reference page, queued in the calendar rather than lost in a spreadsheet.
