# The monthly Google Business Profile maintenance

**Area:** Local SEO · **Cadence:** monthly · **Time:** 45 min

**Trigger.** First week of the month, or as soon as three new reviews arrive.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Copy your profile as it appears: categories, hours, services, description, photos count, latest posts, Q and A. Paste it with the same for your 3 nearest competitors. | `gbp-audit` | A gap list: missing categories, stale hours, thin services, unanswered questions. |
| 2 | Fix the gaps in Google Business Profile: hours, categories, services, a new post if you have news, and answer the open questions. | manual | An updated profile with a change log. |
| 3 | Paste the new reviews with stars and text. Ask for a reply per review that names the specific point raised and never copies a template. | `review-response-writer` | One reply per review, negative ones flagged for your edit. |
| 4 | Edit the flagged replies yourself, post all replies, and log the month's review count and average rating. | manual | Replies posted and a monthly rating log entry. |

**Result.** A profile that stays complete against the competition, every review answered within the month, and a rating log you can read next quarter.
