# The monthly link building sprint

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Cadence:** monthly · **Time:** 4 h

**Trigger.** First week of the month, with a target page chosen from the quick-win table.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Export the referring domains of 3 competitors from Ahrefs or Semrush, plus your own. Paste all four and name the target page. | `link-gap` | Domains linking to two or more competitors and not to you, with the linking page. |
| 2 | Paste the competitor pages that link to dead or redirected resources on your topic. Ask which of your pages could replace each broken target. | `broken-link-prospector` | A list of broken links with your replacement page per link. |
| 3 | Merge both lists, keep 30 prospects with an editorial contact you can find, and note the exact page and reason per prospect. | manual | A prospect sheet of 30 rows with page, contact and reason. |
| 4 | Paste the prospect sheet and your target page. Ask for one short email per prospect, referencing their page and the specific gap or broken link. | `outreach-writer` | 30 personalised emails, under 120 words each. |
| 5 | Send in batches of 10 a day, log replies in the sheet, and follow up once after 7 days. Record every link won with its date. | manual | The sheet updated with replies and links won. |

**Result.** A 30-prospect sheet built from competitor gaps and broken links, 30 sent emails, and a reply log that tells you next month's hit rate.
