# The per-page citable version

**Area:** AI search visibility · **Cadence:** per new page · **Time:** 40 min

**Trigger.** A page is published and its target query is one people ask AI engines, not only Google.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paste the published page and the three questions it answers. Ask for one self-contained passage per question, under 80 words, with the key fact in the first sentence. | `answer-block-writer` | Three passages with the heading each should sit under. |
| 2 | Paste the passages and the page. Ask whether the product, company and people are named the same way as everywhere else on the site. | `entity-consistency` | Naming fixes, or a confirmation that the page matches. |
| 3 | Insert the passages under their headings in the CMS and apply the naming fixes. Do not change anything else on the page. | manual | The page updated with three citable passages. |
| 4 | Four weeks later, run the three questions in the AI engines you track and paste the answers with their sources. | `citation-checker` | Cited or not per question, and who was cited instead. |

**Result.** Every new page ships with three passages an AI engine can quote, named consistently, and a four-week check that feeds the monthly citation table.
