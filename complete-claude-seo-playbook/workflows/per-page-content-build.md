# The per-page content build

**Area:** Content strategy and briefs · **Cadence:** per new page · **Time:** 4 h

**Trigger.** A brief is approved in the calendar and a target query has been assigned to the page.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Give it the target query and the country. Paste the People Also Ask questions and any forum or support tickets you have on the topic. | `question-harvester` | A list of real questions grouped by sub-topic. |
| 2 | Paste the top 10 ranking URLs for the query and their H2s. Ask for the outline that covers what ranks plus the questions nothing yet answers. | `outline-from-serp` | An H2 and H3 outline with a note on what each section must contain. |
| 3 | Merge the outline into the brief and mark the two sections where you have first-hand data or a customer example nobody else has. | `brief-builder` | An approved brief with the outline and the information gain sections marked. |
| 4 | Hand it the approved brief and write the draft section by section. Ask it to leave a placeholder wherever it lacks a fact rather than guess. | `first-draft` | A full draft with placeholders for missing facts. |
| 5 | Fill the placeholders yourself, then paste the draft with your author bio, the data source and any screenshots you took. | `eeat-injector` | The draft with experience signals, citations and an author block added. |
| 6 | Publish in the CMS, submit the URL for indexing in Search Console, and add the page to the linking workflow's queue. | manual | A live URL queued for internal linking. |

**Result.** A published page built from real questions and the ranking outline, with your own data in it, already queued for internal links and a 28-day recheck.
