# The per-page link it in routine

**Area:** Internal linking · **Cadence:** per new page · **Time:** 30 min

**Trigger.** A new page is published and sits in the linking queue from the content workflow.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Give it the new URL, its target query and your Search Console pages export. Ask for existing pages that already rank for related queries and should link to it. | `link-opportunity-finder` | 5 to 10 source pages with the sentence where the link fits. |
| 2 | Paste the new page's cluster from the keyword universe. Ask whether it is the hub or a spoke, and which links must exist in both directions. | `hub-spoke-builder` | The hub, the spokes and a list of required links with anchors. |
| 3 | Add the links in the CMS on each source page and on the new page itself. Use the anchors given, not the page title. | manual | Links live on every source page. |
| 4 | Two weeks later, check the URL's indexing status in Search Console and its first impressions in the performance export. | manual | Indexed yes or no, and first impressions count. |

**Result.** Every new page gets 5 to 10 contextual internal links and its place in a hub within a day of publishing, with a two-week indexing check to close the loop.
