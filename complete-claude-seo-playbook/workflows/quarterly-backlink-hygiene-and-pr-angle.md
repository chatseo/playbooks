# The quarterly backlink hygiene and PR angle

**Area:** Link building and digital PR · **Cadence:** quarterly · **Time:** 3 h

**Trigger.** Every quarter, or when a sudden spike of new referring domains appears in Search Console links.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Export your full backlink profile from Ahrefs or Semrush and the Search Console links report. Paste both. | `toxic-link-triage` | Links sorted into ignore, watch and disavow, with the reason per domain. |
| 2 | Review the disavow bucket by hand. Only keep domains you are sure are manipulative, then update the disavow file if you use one. | manual | A reviewed disavow list, or a note that none is needed. |
| 3 | Paste a table from your own data: usage numbers, customer survey answers, or aggregated results. Ask for the three story angles a journalist could run. | `digital-pr-angle` | Three angles with the headline, the number behind it and the audience. |
| 4 | Pick one angle, publish the data page, then paste the page and a list of 20 journalists or blogs that covered similar stories. | `outreach-writer` | 20 pitches tailored to each outlet's past coverage. |
| 5 | Send the pitches, track coverage and links in a sheet, and file the other two angles for next quarter. | manual | A coverage log and two angles in reserve. |

**Result.** A cleaned backlink profile you reviewed yourself, one data story published and pitched, and two more angles ready so next quarter starts with a pitch, not a search.
