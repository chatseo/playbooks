# The quarterly keyword universe rebuild

**Area:** Keyword research and clustering · **Cadence:** quarterly · **Time:** 3 h

**Trigger.** First week of each quarter, before the content calendar for the next three months is set.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Give it your 5 to 10 seed terms, the product category and the countries you sell in, plus last quarter's Search Console query export. | `seed-expander` | A keyword universe of several hundred terms, tagged by source and modifier. |
| 2 | Paste the universe together with any volume column you have from Ahrefs or Semrush. If there is no volume column, say so and it ranks by count only. | `cluster-builder` | Topic clusters with a head term, member queries and one target page each. |
| 3 | Run the cluster head terms through so each cluster gets one dominant intent and a note when the SERP is mixed. | `intent-classifier` | The same clusters with an intent label and a page type. |
| 4 | Pick the 5 to 8 clusters you can actually cover this quarter. Check each head term against your existing pages so you refresh rather than duplicate. | manual | A shortlist of clusters, each marked new page or refresh. |
| 5 | For each new-page cluster, hand the head term, member queries and intent label over and ask for a brief. | `brief-builder` | One brief per new page, ready for the content workflow. |

**Result.** A quarter's worth of briefs mapped to clusters and intents, dropped into the content calendar with the cluster table saved for next quarter's diff.
