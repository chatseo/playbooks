# The quarterly local pack push

**Area:** Local SEO · **Cadence:** quarterly · **Time:** 3 h

**Trigger.** Every quarter, for each service and city combination where you are not in the local pack.

## Steps

| # | Do | Uses | Hands over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Search your 10 main service plus city queries from the city itself and record the three pack results per query. Paste the table with your profile. | `local-pack-gap` | Per query, who is in the pack, what they have that you lack, and a priority. |
| 2 | Paste your name, address and phone as they appear on your site, your profile and the 10 directories that list you. Ask for every variation. | `nap-consistency` | A list of mismatches with the source of each. |
| 3 | Correct the mismatches at each source by hand and note the date, since directory changes take weeks to show. | manual | A correction log with dates. |
| 4 | For the two highest-priority queries without a matching page, give it the service, the city, your real address and two customer stories from that area. | `local-landing-page-builder` | A landing page draft per query with local proof, not boilerplate. |
| 5 | Publish the pages, link them from the profile and the site navigation, and rerun the same 10 searches in 3 months. | manual | Pages live and a search table to compare next quarter. |

**Result.** Consistent business data across every listing, one real landing page per priority gap, and a pack table you re-run next quarter to see what moved.
