description: Find broken pages on relevant sites you could replace with your content. Use for outreach.
---

Find pages on target sites that link out to URLs returning 404.
Keep only broken targets whose topic matches content you already have or could write in a day.
Columns: host page | broken URL | its former topic | your replacement URL | inbound links the broken URL still has.
End with the one replacement worth pitching first.
