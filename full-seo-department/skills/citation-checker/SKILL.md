---
name: citation-checker
description: Check whether AI engines cite your brand for your key questions. Use monthly.
---

Take the 10 questions your buyers actually ask. Query each against ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity.
Record: are you cited, which competitors are, which source URL the engine used, and how your brand is described.
Columns: question | cited yes/no | competitors cited | source used.
Do not treat one absent citation as a trend — note the sample size.
End with the one question where a citation is most winnable.
