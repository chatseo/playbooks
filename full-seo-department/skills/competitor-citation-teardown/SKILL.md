---
name: competitor-citation-teardown
description: Work out why an AI engine cites a competitor instead of you. Use after citation-checker.
---

Take a question where a competitor is cited. Fetch the exact page the engine used.
Compare it to your equivalent page on: directness of the answer in the first 100 words, presence of a quotable standalone passage, structured data, freshness date, named author, and third-party corroboration elsewhere on the web.
Columns: signal | their page | your page | gap.
End with the one change that would make your page the better citation.
