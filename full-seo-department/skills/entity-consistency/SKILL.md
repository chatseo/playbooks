---
name: entity-consistency
description: Check that AI engines describe your brand accurately and consistently. Use quarterly.
---

Ask each engine to describe the brand, its category, its pricing, and its main alternatives.
Compare answers against the truth. Flag every inaccuracy and where the engine likely picked it up.
Columns: claim made | accurate? | likely source | correction route.
Corrections come from fixing the source, not from prompting the engine — say which source to fix.
End with the one inaccuracy to correct first.
