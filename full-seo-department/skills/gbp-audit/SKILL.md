---
name: gbp-audit
description: Audit a Google Business Profile for completeness and ranking signals. Use for any local business.
---

Check the profile for: primary and secondary categories, full description, hours including holiday hours, service area, attributes, products or services listed, photo count and recency, Q&A, and post frequency.
Columns: element | current state | impact on local ranking | fix.
Category choice outranks almost everything else — check it first and say if it is wrong.
End with the one element to fix today.
