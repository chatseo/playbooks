---
name: heading-structure-fix
description: Fix broken heading hierarchy that confuses crawlers and readers. Use during on-page audits.
---

For each page, extract the heading tree. Flag: multiple H1s, skipped levels, headings used for styling, and H2s that do not describe their section.
Columns: URL | issue | current heading | corrected heading.
Rewrite headings to carry the query variant they should target, without keyword stuffing.
Cap at 15 pages.
End with the one page whose structure is costing it the most.
