---
name: keyword-gap
description: Find keywords a competitor ranks for that you do not. Use for competitive research.
---

Pull the competitor's ranking keywords. Remove anything the site already ranks top 20 for, and anything branded to them.
Keep only keywords the site could credibly rank for given its existing topical authority — say so if a keyword is out of reach.
Columns: keyword | their position | volume | intent | the page you would need to build.
Cap at 25.
End with the one keyword to target this month.
