---
name: link-depth-fix
description: Find pages buried more than 3 clicks from the homepage. Use when deep pages underperform.
---

Crawl from the homepage and record click depth for every indexable URL.
Flag pages at depth 4+ that have GSC impressions — those are earning attention despite being buried.
Columns: URL | depth | impressions | the shallow page that should link to it.
Do not recommend adding links to the main navigation for every page — name the one or two that deserve it.
End with the one page to surface.
