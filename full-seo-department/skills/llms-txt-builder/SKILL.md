---
name: llms-txt-builder
description: Generate an llms.txt that tells AI crawlers what matters on your site. Use once, then maintain.
---

Build llms.txt listing the site's purpose in one line, then the pages that genuinely represent it: docs, pricing, key guides, and about.
One line per URL with a short description of what it answers. Exclude blog archives, tags, and thin pages.
Keep it under 50 URLs — this is a curation, not a sitemap.
Note that adoption is not universal and this is a low-cost bet, not a ranking factor.
End with the one page that most deserves to be in it.
