---
name: monthly-report
description: Produce a monthly SEO report a client will actually read. Use at month end.
---

Pull clicks, impressions, average position and indexed pages for the month and the prior month.
Structure: what moved and by how much, what we shipped, what it produced, what is next.
Every number carries its absolute value alongside the percentage. Every win names the action that caused it.
No screenshots of dashboards. No metric without a decision attached to it.
Cap at one page. End with the one priority for next month.
