description: Find name, address and phone inconsistencies across the web. Use before any local campaign.

Collect the business NAP as listed on the site, GBP, and the major directories and aggregators.
Compare against the canonical version character by character — abbreviations, suite numbers, and phone formats all count.
Columns: source | name | address | phone | mismatch | how to correct it.
End with the one citation to correct first, weighted by the directory's authority.
