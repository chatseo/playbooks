name: position-8-15-finder
description: Find pages sitting at position 8-15 that deserve a push. Use first — this is the fastest win on the list.
---

Pull GSC queries from the last 28 days with average position between 8 and 15 and impressions above 100.
Rank by impressions multiplied by the CTR you would gain moving to position 3.
Columns: URL | query | position | impressions | estimated clicks gained | the one change to make.
The change must be specific: a section to add, a link to build, a title to rewrite. Not "improve the content".
Cap at 10 rows. End with the single page to push first.
