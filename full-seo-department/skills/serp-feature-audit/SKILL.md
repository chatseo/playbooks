---
name: serp-feature-audit
description: Find SERP features you could realistically win. Use to capture clicks without moving position.
---

For each target keyword, list which SERP features appear: featured snippet, PAA, image pack, video, local pack, AI Overview.
For each feature the site does not hold, judge whether it is winnable given current position — anything outside the top 10 is not.
Columns: keyword | feature | current holder | your position | the specific change needed to win it.
Cap at 15.
End with the one feature to go after.
