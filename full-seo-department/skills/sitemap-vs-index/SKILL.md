name: sitemap-vs-index
description: Find sitemap URLs Google has not indexed, and say why. Use when new pages are not appearing in search.
---

Diff the sitemap URL set against GSC indexed URLs.
For each missing URL, classify the reason: discovered-not-crawled, crawled-not-indexed, duplicate, soft 404, or blocked.
Group by reason and count. Show 3 example URLs per reason, not all of them.
Crawled-not-indexed means thin or duplicate content — say so plainly rather than suggesting a resubmit.
End with the one reason group to fix.
