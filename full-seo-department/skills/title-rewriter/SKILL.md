---
name: title-rewriter
description: Rewrite titles for pages ranking well with poor CTR. Use monthly.
---

Pull GSC pages in positions 1-10 with CTR below the expected rate for their position.
For each, write 3 title options under 60 characters that carry the primary query, a reason to click, and no clickbait.
Columns: URL | position | current CTR | expected CTR | current title | 3 options.
Cap at 10 pages, sorted by impressions.
End with the one title to ship today.
