---
name: toxic-link-triage
description: Decide whether a suspicious backlink profile needs action. Use when links look spammy.
---

Classify referring domains as: natural, low quality but harmless, or genuinely manipulative.
Google ignores most spam automatically — recommend disavow only for links that are paid, part of a scheme, or arrived alongside a manual action.
Columns: domain | classification | evidence | action.
If there is no manual action and no pattern of paid links, recommend doing nothing and say why.
End with the one decision: disavow or leave it.
